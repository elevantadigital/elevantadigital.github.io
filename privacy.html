Privacy notice

How we handle your information.

Effective November 2026. This notice describes the project inquiry form and basic website operation.

Information you provide

When you submit our project questionnaire, we collect the information you enter, which may include your name, business name, email, phone number, business type, requested service, preferred timeline, and project details.

Why we use it

We use inquiry information to review your request, communicate with you, prepare quotes, and maintain records of business inquiries. Submitting the form does not subscribe you to a marketing mailing list.

Service providers

Our site is hosted using GitHub Pages. Form submissions are processed and stored by Formspree, which sends notifications to our business email provider. Those providers may process technical information under their own privacy policies. We do not collect payment card information through the questionnaire.

Cookies and analytics

We have not intentionally installed advertising trackers or a separate analytics service on this site. Hosting and form providers may use essential technical data or cookies for service delivery, security, and abuse prevention.

Retention and access

We retain inquiry information only as reasonably needed to respond, manage our business records, and meet applicable obligations. You may ask us to review, correct, or delete information you submitted, subject to legitimate retention needs.

Your choices

You can contact us without using the form by emailing elevantadigital.business@gmail.com. Please do not submit passwords, financial account details, or sensitive personal information.

Changes

We may update this notice if our services or information practices change. The current version will appear on this page.

This notice should be reviewed against actual business practices and applicable privacy requirements before accepting customers.
