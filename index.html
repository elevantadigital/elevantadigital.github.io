Common questions

Before we get started.

Is the quote request free?

Yes. Completing the project questionnaire does not obligate you to purchase anything.

Are domain and hosting fees included?

Not automatically. Any third-party fees will be identified in your written quote. You should retain control of your domain and accounts.

How long will my website take?

Typical targets are 7–10 business days for Starter and 10–15 for Business after we receive all necessary materials. The agreed scope and availability can affect timing.

Can you guarantee more customers or top search rankings?

No. We design useful websites and implement basic on-page SEO, but results depend on many factors outside our control.

What do you need from me?

Usually your business details, service descriptions, contact information, logo if available, and permission to use any photos or other materials you provide.

What happens before I pay?

We provide a written scope and agreement identifying deliverables, payment terms, revisions, and responsibilities before accepting a paid project.
