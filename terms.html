Service information

Clear expectations from the start.

This page explains how inquiries and published package information work. It is not a substitute for the individual written agreement signed before a paid project.

Quotes and commitments

Submitting an inquiry is free and creates no obligation to purchase. Published prices are starting rates, not binding offers. We confirm project scope, deliverables, fees, payment schedule, timeline, and responsibilities in a written quote and agreement before work begins.

Packages and revisions

The Starter package generally covers one responsive page. The Business package generally covers up to five responsive pages. Both list two revision rounds, with exact boundaries and exclusions defined in the agreement. Additional pages, features, integrations, or changes may require a separate quote.

Content and accounts

Clients are responsible for providing accurate business information and materials they have permission to use. Domain registration, hosting, and third-party subscriptions are not included unless the written quote says otherwise. Clients should maintain ownership and control of their own domain and service accounts.

Timelines and results

Published delivery windows are estimates beginning after required materials and approvals are received. We do not promise particular search rankings, traffic, lead counts, sales, or uninterrupted website availability.

Website Care

Optional Website Care is subject to a separate agreement that defines supported websites, monthly work limits, response expectations, billing, cancellation, and exclusions.

Payments, ownership and cancellation

These matters are determined by the individual signed agreement. No payments are accepted through this website's project questionnaire.
